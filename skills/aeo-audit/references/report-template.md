# AEO audit report template

Use this template for a durable audit report. Keep it proportional to the observed evidence and remove unused optional sections. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03) [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)

## Executive summary

State the overall deterministic readiness without a numeric score, up to three priority gaps, up to three verified strengths, the main coverage limit, and the smallest next action. If no material gap was observed, say so directly. [C04](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#c04) [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)

## Scope and method

Record the brand, canonical domain, buyer questions, named answer surfaces, sampled page types, evidence level, collection methods, and access constraints. [P01](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p01) [C11](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#c11)

## Priority gaps

Give every gap its own subsection using the fields below; do not compress a finding into a pipe-delimited paragraph. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03)

### F001 — Short finding title

- **State:** Observed or inferred. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03)
- **Severity:** High, medium, or low. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03)
- **Confidence:** High, medium, or low, with uncertainty reflected in the evidence. [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)
- **Effort:** Small, medium, or large estimate; identify the responsible dependency when known. [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)
- **Surface:** Named product surface or access mode. [C01](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#c01)
- **Location:** Exact URL, page type, response, or file. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03)
- **Evidence:** Observed passage, property, or measurement with ledger IDs. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03)
- **Impact:** Bounded effect on retrieval, understanding, evidence quality, accessibility, or the supplied buyer question. [C04](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#c04)
- **Recommendation:** Smallest action that addresses the evidence; do not promise visibility. [C04](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#c04)
- **Verification:** Observable check that would confirm or disconfirm the change. [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)

## Verified strengths

Keep positive observations out of the gap list. Give each a stable ID, surface, location, evidence, and preservation or regression check; do not assign severity. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03)

## Untested checks and coverage limits

State each material unknown, why it was unavailable, and the exact evidence needed to resolve it. Never convert an unavailable check into a pass or failure. [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)

## Evidence ledger

For each artifact record its stable ID, URL or file, retrieval time when relevant, method, authentication state, crawler identity basis, source ownership, and sampled or exhaustive coverage. [P03](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p03) [C11](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#c11)

## Smallest next experiment

Name the single follow-up most likely to change the conclusions, including the evidence it would produce. [P07](https://github.com/aeokit-dev/aeo-skills/blob/v0.1.1/research/report-source.md#p07)
